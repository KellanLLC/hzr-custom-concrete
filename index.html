What we pour

Eight things we do, and do properly.

Residential and commercial. Every photograph below is one of our own jobs in Ventura County, with no stock photography anywhere on this page. The crew that quotes your job is the crew that pours it.

Driveways & Aprons

Broken out, regraded, formed and poured on a compacted base, then jointed so it cracks on our line and not its own.

Patios & Back Yards

Whole yards taken from dirt to a finished slab, scored on a grid and pitched so the water leaves the house.

Walkways & Sidewalks

Paths and public sidewalk cut to the curve of the bed, formed in place and finished to match what is already there.

Steps & Pool Decks

Risers cast in one pour with the edges run straight, and deck surfaces finished for wet feet rather than for a photo.

Retaining Walls

Formed, braced and tied out in steel before anything is poured, because a wall that moves takes the yard with it.

Footings & Foundations

Trenches dug to depth, formed and tied for additions, garages and new structures. The part nobody sees again.

Custom & Stamped Finishes

Broom, smooth trowel, salt, stamped flagstone, integral colour and sealer. The finish is what you look at for years.

Demolition & Repair

Old slab broken out and hauled off, or patched where a patch will genuinely hold. We will tell you which one it is.

The forty-year decision

Everything that matters happens before the truck arrives.

A finished slab hides its own workmanship, so here is the half you would never see. Anyone can pour concrete. This is the difference between forty years and four.

Grade and base

Broken out, regraded and compacted before a single form goes down. Every slab that fails, failed here first; it just took four years to show.

A full mat of steel

Tied out across the whole pad, not one strip down the middle. It is among the cheapest parts of the job and the only one you cannot add later.

Joints on our line

Concrete cracks. Ours cracks where we tooled it to, on a straight line you were shown before the pour, instead of diagonally across your driveway.
